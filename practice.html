Practice Problems

Pick a topic and get a short analytical problem with new numbers every time. Type your answers to have them checked, and ask for a hint when you are stuck.

Choose a topic

Every problem is generated with random numbers, so you can practice the same idea as many times as you like. Start with Easy (one idea, direct use of a formula), move on to Medium (a property or two steps), and finish with Challenging (several steps or a twist). Work it out on paper first, then enter your answers. Each problem has a code; share the link with your TA or classmates to show them exactly the same problem.

DIFFICULTY

How to enter answers

You can type a number (0.25, -3) or an exact expression. Exact expressions are best. Decimals are accepted when they are within about 0.5% of the answer, so give at least three significant digits.

  • Imaginary unit: j (or i), e.g. 2-3j, -j/2, (1+j)/4
  • Constants and functions: pi, e, sqrt(2), exp(-j*pi/4), e^(-2), sin(pi/3), cos, abs, angle, atan, ln
  • Multiplication can be implicit: 2pi, 3j, 2(1-j)
  • Phases are in radians. Any equivalent angle (adding a multiple of 2π) is accepted.

The line under each box shows how your input was read, so check it before you press Check (or Enter).
