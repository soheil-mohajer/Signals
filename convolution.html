Convolution Explorer

Flip. Shift. Multiply. Sum. Watch the output of an LTI system build up one time instant at a time.

Continuous-time convolution

\( y(t) = (x*h)(t) = \displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x(\tau)\,h(t-\tau)\,d\tau \)
\( y[n] = (x*h)[n] = \displaystyle\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty} x[k]\,h[n-k] \)

Input x(t)

Impulse response h(t)

1Flip and shift: x(τ) and h(t − τ)

h(τ) is reversed in time, then slid so that its origin sits at τ = t. Drag on any plot to move t.

2Multiply: x(τ) · h(t − τ)

Only where the two signals overlap is the product nonzero. Its signed area is the output value.

3Integrate: y(t) is the area of the product

Each value of t gives one point of the output.
Tip: convolution is commutative, \(x*h=h*x\). When computing by hand, flip whichever signal makes the overlap limits easier to find.
