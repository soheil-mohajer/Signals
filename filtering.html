Filtering with RLC circuits

Every circuit below is an LTI system with frequency response \(H(j\omega)\). A periodic input with Fourier series coefficients \(a_k\) produces a periodic output whose coefficients are simply scaled by the frequency response at each harmonic.

\( x(t)=\displaystyle\sum_k a_k e^{jk\omega_0 t} \;\longrightarrow\; \boxed{H(j\omega)} \;\longrightarrow\; y(t)=\displaystyle\sum_k \underbrace{H(jk\omega_0)\,a_k}_{b_k}\, e^{jk\omega_0 t} \)

Why no line spectrum?

A unit step is not periodic, so it has no Fourier series. Its frequency content is spread continuously over all frequencies (that is the Fourier transform, coming later in the course). The time-domain plot shows the step response: the integral of the impulse response \(h(t)\).
