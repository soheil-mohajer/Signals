Continuous-Time Fourier Transform

Pick a signal, transform it in time, and see how its spectrum X(ω) responds, with no integrals to grind through.

CTFT Explorer

The CTFT describes an aperiodic signal x(t) as a continuum of complex exponentials ejωt. A few basic pairs plus the properties (shift, reversal, scaling, modulation, symmetry) give the transform of many other signals without integrating. Try predicting each spectrum before you look.

\( X(\omega)=\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x(t)\,e^{-j\omega t}\,dt, \qquad x(t)=\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} X(\omega)\,e^{j\omega t}\,d\omega \)

Input signal x(t)

Use t, u(t), rect(t) (1 for |t| < ½), tri(t) (1 − |t| for |t| < 1), pi, j and functions such as cos, sin, exp, abs. Example: t e^(-t^2). The signal is evaluated for −30 ≤ t ≤ 30.
