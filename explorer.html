Define the Fourier series

Complex exponential Fourier series:

\[\widetilde{x}_N(t)=\sum_{k=-N}^{N}a_k e^{j\frac{2\pi}{T}kt}\]

Sawtooth wave: choose Custom to enter your own Fourier coefficients.

Use formulas in k for positive and negative integer indices. For a real-valued signal, choose coefficients with \(a_{-k}=a_k^*\).

For an odd number M, enter all M coefficients from \(a_{-(M-1)/2}\) to \(a_{(M-1)/2}\).

Drag a stem up or down to change that coefficient. The Fourier reconstruction updates immediately. You can also type precise values in the table.

Define the discrete-time Fourier series

Discrete-time Fourier series (only N harmonics are distinct):

\[\widetilde{x}_M[n]=\sum_{\substack{k\in\langle N\rangle\\ |k|\le M}}a_k e^{j\frac{2\pi}{N}kn}\]\[a_k=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=\langle N\rangle}x[n]\,e^{-j\frac{2\pi}{N}kn}\]

Use n and N. The N coefficients are computed exactly from these samples.

Use k and N. The formulas are evaluated for the N indices \(k=-\lfloor\frac{N-1}{2}\rfloor,\dots,\lfloor\frac{N}{2}\rfloor\); every other k repeats with period N, since \(a_{k+N}=a_k\).

Drag a stem up or down to change that coefficient. There is one stem for each of the N distinct coefficients. You can also type precise values in the table.
