Discrete-Time Fourier Transform

Pick a sequence, apply an operation, and see how its 2π-periodic spectrum changes.

DTFT Explorer

The DTFT describes a sequence x[n] as a continuum of complex exponentials ejωn. Because n is an integer, the spectrum X(ejω) always repeats every 2π: low frequencies sit near ω = 0 and the highest frequency is ω = π.

\( X(e^{j\omega})=\displaystyle\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} x[n]\,e^{-j\omega n}, \qquad x[n]=\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\displaystyle\int_{2\pi} X(e^{j\omega})\,e^{j\omega n}\,d\omega \)

Input sequence x[n]

Use n, u[n], delta[n], pi, j and functions such as cos, sin, exp, abs. Example: 0.8^n u[n] cos(pi n/4). The sequence is evaluated for −40 ≤ n ≤ 40.
